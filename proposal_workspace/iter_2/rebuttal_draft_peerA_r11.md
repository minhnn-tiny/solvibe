# Rebuttal to the iteration 2 meta-review

Authors: Peer A (Claude Code) and Peer B (Codex). Peer A drafted this rebuttal and the revised proposal `proposal_v12.md`. Peer B's corrections from Loop 1 are applied before the committee sees it. It answers every required change in `iter_2/meta_review.md`.

**Stagnation rule.** Five items repeat iteration-1 items after a fix was attempted: item 1 (iteration-1 item 7), item 2 (item 1), item 4 (item 8), item 6 (item 16) and item 9 (item 13). Each gets a structurally different fix, not a refinement:

- design comparison (item 1): it was a secondary report; it is now the principal estimand, with a stated smallest difference, a precision target, an allocation rule with a two-phase design (protocols that contribute V functions only), and a stated fallback if the target is unaffordable;
- feasibility (item 2): earlier fixes changed the size of a fixed design and listed assumed hours; now the study processes protocols in a random order until fixed dates, so it finishes on time whatever the throughput, and only its precision depends on throughput; the automated steps are measured now;
- extension contrast (item 4): earlier, one common outcome; now a matched outcome (the behaviour each wrong assertion endorses), matched contrary evidence, a log-odds contrast, and the experiment is moved out of the claimed contributions;
- 63008 (item 6): earlier, scheduled; now built and run;
- unresolved outcomes (item 9): earlier, a bounding rule only; now the definition is narrowed, power under the rule is simulated, and a launch criterion is set.

**What we could not do.** Item 2 asks for development cases with a person doing every human-review step. Those hours can only come from the student and from paid people working on real cases. We did not produce them, and we do not present LLM timings as human ones. The structural fix above is our answer to the feasibility objection: the twelve-month deliverable no longer depends on those hours, and the claims that survive at each throughput are fixed in advance.

**New evidence since iteration 2** (every file is hashed in `tools/MANIFEST.md`):

- `evidence/63008/`: the complete executable evidence package for finding 63008;
- `evidence/frame_count/`: fix scope of the 43 pilot findings with public history, and per-protocol counts;
- `evidence/timings.md`: measured times of the automated work;
- `tools/power_sim_v12.py`, `v13`, `v14`, `v15`, `v16` and their result files.

## Disagreements among reviewers

1. Paper 2's contrast: we follow the majority and the AC (item 4).
2. Outcome for the contrast: the endorsed-behaviour outcome, with vulnerability retention kept within the security arms (item 4).
3. Does paper 2 matter for acceptance: it becomes a specified extension with no claimed contribution.
4. Tested or estimated: estimated against a precision target, with R3's reading rule (items 1 and 14).
5. How much feasibility evidence: automated steps measured; human hours not yet; the time box makes completion independent of them (items 2 and 5).
6. More precision or less work: two attempts instead of three, and protocols that contribute V functions only, before any increase in effort (items 1 and 2).
7. What detection validation measures: both the auditor plan and the audit-missed set (items 7 and 8).
8. Equal search: settled for the primary label; confirmation (item 3) and validation (items 7 and 8) now addressed.
9. Ratings: no disagreement.
