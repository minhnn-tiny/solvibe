# Rebuttal to the iteration 1 meta-review

Authors: Peer A (Claude Code) and Peer B (Codex). Peer A drafted this rebuttal; Peer B's amendments are marked [Peer B]. It answers every required change in `iter_1/meta_review.md`. The revised proposal (proposal_v7 and later) will be produced in Loop 1 with this rebuttal as the agenda.

**Stagnation rule.** Two Blocking issues (feasibility, item 1; equal search for flaws, item 2) were raised in Loop 1 (points P15 and N2/P12) and fixed there, and the committee raised them again. The fixes below are therefore structurally different from the earlier ones, not refinements of them:
- feasibility: the earlier fix kept the full design and added a month-two gate and a reduction order; the new fix replaces the design with a small core study and moves everything else to named extensions and a second paper;
- equal search: the earlier fix matched discovery budgets and replayed PoVs across authors; the new fix changes the primary endpoint so that it counts only flaws found by one common procedure that never sees the audit, and measures that procedure's recall for each author with seeded flaws.

## Disagreements among reviewers

1. Detection direction: measured, not argued (item 2); three estimates side by side.
2. Primary endpoint: D detected by the common procedure (item 5).
3. AI-assisted functions: the AC's threshold rule (item 3).
4. Shrink or add: the costed plan decides; additions are extensions unless they fit (item 1).
5. Security-specific RQ2: R4's matched control is confirmatory; R2's trajectory analysis is secondary (item 8).
6. Counting natural fault-enforcing tests: R5's full month-one count (item 9).
7. Ratings: majority view accepted; items 7 and 21 for novelty, items 1 to 3 for soundness.
