Corrections (added by Peer A after the run, no simulated number changed):
1. The first line says "certainty protocols outside the jackknife". The run used JK = "all": every sampled protocol, certain or not, is deleted in turn, as in V33 (see the script's docstring).
2. The lines "share of studies with at least one certainty protocol 0.000" are wrong. With JK = "all" the script measured whether fewer than 22 protocols entered the jackknife, which never happens. Counted directly from the frames: frames of 50 protocols have 2 to 11 certainty protocols (median 6), frames of 70 have 0 to 6 (median 3); a certainty protocol is selected in every study of its frame.
3. In the low-V-yield frames the undefined shares (0.171 to 0.268) and the low coverage bounds (0.710 to 0.800) come from samples with no built V function: the estimator then multiplies a V share of 0 by an undefined V rate. power_sim_v40.py defines the V contribution as 0 in that case.
